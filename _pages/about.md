He currently does research at [Stanford's Institute for Human Centered AI]([https://www.reddit.com](https://hai.stanford.edu)) and the [Language Data and Reasoning Lab](https://ldr.stanford.edu). Link to your favorite [subreddit](https://www.reddit.com).
